Moon Fire Evidence Map

An evidence map of NASA's microgravity combustion experiments that ranks investigations by how much they tell us about fire in a Moon-habitat atmosphere, and shows exactly where the evidence stops.

Atmosphere Journey

Walk from Earth to the expected Moon habitat to a spacesuit and see how many recorded tests exist near each atmosphere.

Evidence Flame: ring colours are the outcome shares of the tests in the window; brightness is the share with a recorded outcome; sharpness is evidence quality. Not a prediction.
